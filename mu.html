Bull Case

  • AI Memory Supercycle is StructuralEvery GPU needs massive HBM/DRAM/NAND. $660-690B hyperscaler capex in 2026 alone. This is infrastructure buildout, not a fad.
  • HBM Sold Out Through 2027Non-cancellable, fixed-price contracts provide unprecedented revenue visibility. This IS structurally different from spot-market cycles.
  • 81% Gross Margin is UnprecedentedNever in Micron's history. Reflects pricing power + product mix shift to high-value HBM/server DRAM. Not just a cycle peak metric.
  • Forward P/E of ~4.4x is Absurdly CheapSemi industry avg is 40.9x. Even applying a harsh cyclical discount, MU is mispriced. PEG ratio of 0.2 vs fair value of 1.0.
  • $90+ EPS Expected FY2027At just 6x earnings, that's $540. At 10x (still below semi avg), that's $900. Current price implies earnings crater 80%+ — unlikely before 2028.
  • Supply Constraints Persist Until 2028New fabs take years. Industry supply growth of ~16% is well below AI-driven demand growth. No capacity glut imminent.
  • Oligopoly StructureOnly 3 companies make DRAM/HBM globally. Consolidation from 23 companies in 1986. Rational supply discipline is more likely than historical periods.
  • NAND Recovery Adds Another Growth LegEnterprise SSD pricing up >25% sequentially. G9 node ramping. NAND was 20% of revenue and accelerating.

Bear Case (SA Article + Other)

  • Cyclicality is Eternal (SA Core Thesis)Memory has been cyclical for 40+ years through every "secular" demand driver (PCs, mobile, cloud). EV/Sales at historical peak of 3.18x sector-wide.
  • EV/Sales at 8.11x TTM is Historically ExtremePrevious cycle peaks for MU were at much lower multiples. Reversion to mean implies >50% downside.
  • Google TurboQuant / Memory Efficiency6x compression of KV cache memory. Counter: this mainly affects inference DRAM, NOT HBM for training. Overstated risk but worth monitoring.
  • Tariff ExposureMemory modules/SSDs (not bare chips) subject to tariffs. Could dampen consumer demand and create pricing pressure. Stock already -14% on tariff news.
  • Post-2027 Cliff RiskWhen Samsung/SK Hynix capacity expansions complete and if AI spending moderates, pricing could normalize rapidly.
  • China Market LossExited Chinese server market after government ban. Further retaliatory actions could impact remaining mobile/auto business.
  • Customer ConcentrationHeavy reliance on NVIDIA ecosystem and a few hyperscalers. Any slowdown in their spending directly impacts Micron.
  • Long-Term Contracts ≠ Long-Term DemandSA author argues contracts exist because of high prices, not the reverse. Customers can renegotiate or reduce future orders.

Our Counterarguments

On EV/Sales: TTM EV/Sales is misleading when revenue is tripling. FWD EV/Sales of 4.11x is high but justified by 81% margins (historically 30-40%).

On cyclicality: Valid historically, but the industry consolidated from 23 to 3 players. Oligopoly pricing discipline + multi-year contracts are genuinely new structural features.

On "this time is different": It doesn't need to be permanent — even 2 more years of supercycle earnings at current valuations makes MU a buy. The question is timing, not if.

On contracts: Non-cancellable contracts with fixed pricing ARE a structural change vs spot market. Even if demand softens post-2027, 2026-2027 earnings are locked in.

On PEG ratio: Commenters correctly note PEG of 0.2 — the market is pricing in an 80%+ earnings decline that would need to happen almost immediately to justify current price.

Investment Thesis

Micron is experiencing the most powerful demand environment in the history of the memory industry, driven by an AI infrastructure buildout that has no precedent in scale or duration. The Seeking Alpha bear thesis — that memory is cyclical and current valuations reflect a cycle peak — is historically valid but fails to account for three genuinely structural changes: (1) industry consolidation to 3 players enabling pricing discipline, (2) multi-year non-cancellable contracts providing 2+ years of earnings visibility, and (3) HBM as a new high-margin product category that didn't exist in prior cycles.

At a forward P/E of ~4.4x with 81% gross margins guided and $90+ EPS expected for FY2027, the market is pricing in an immediate and severe earnings collapse. We believe this is premature by at least 18-24 months. Even applying a harsh cyclical discount of 6x to FY2027 EPS of $90 yields a $540 stock — 52% above current levels. The risk-reward is asymmetric to the upside.

Key risk: The bear case is not wrong, just early. A cycle downturn WILL come eventually — likely 2028+ when capacity expansion completes. The correct strategy is to ride the supercycle earnings while they last and tighten stops as we approach late 2027. The current selloff (-25% from ATH) on tariff fears and TurboQuant noise is a gift for those with a 12+ month horizon.

Price Target: $550 (12-month) — based on 6x FY2027E EPS of $90. Conservative multiple reflects cyclical discount. Upside to $700+ if market re-rates to even 8x.
